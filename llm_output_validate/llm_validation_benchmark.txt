METHODOLOGY
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  How this validator works
  ------------------------
  Each LLM-generated clinical report is automatically checked against
  four independent tiers. No human reviewer is required for this pass.
  The validator catches structural, diagnostic, medical, and confidence
  issues — but CANNOT judge prose quality or subtle clinical reasoning.

  Tier 1 — Structural Completeness  (weight 30%)
  ------------------------------------------------
  All 12 required sections must be present and contain >= 20 characters.
  Required sections:
    • QUICK CLINICAL DECISION    • Executive Summary
    • Clinical Presentation      • Imaging Findings
    • Differential Diagnosis     • Pathophysiology
    • Tumor Grading              • Clinical Implications
    • Management Plan            • Next Steps
    • Educational Pearls         • References

  Tier 2 — Diagnostic Consistency  (weight 35%)
  ------------------------------------------------
  The LLM text must mention the DL model's predicted class using at
  least one accepted synonym.  Urgency level must match expected range.

  Class         Accepted synonyms                      Urgency
  glioma        glioma/glioblastoma/GBM/astrocytoma    STAT or Urgent
  meningioma    meningioma/meningeal/dural tail         STAT/Urgent/Routine
  pituitary     pituitary/adenoma/sellar/macroadenoma   STAT/Urgent/Routine
  notumor       no tumor/normal/no evidence             Routine only

  Tier 3 — Medical Plausibility  (weight 25%)
  ---------------------------------------------
  Class-specific medical terms must appear in the report.
  If sentence-transformers is installed, cosine similarity to a
  reference summary is also measured (warn if < 0.30).

  Class        Required terms
  glioma       IDH, MGMT, WHO grade, resection, temozolomide
  meningioma   dural, resection, grade, surgery, seizure
  pituitary    hormone, vision, sellar, prolactin, cortisol
  notumor      normal, no evidence, follow-up, routine

  Tier 4 — Confidence Alignment  (weight 10%)
  ---------------------------------------------
  LLM confidence label extracted and compared to DL numeric score.
  High   → 90–100%   |   Medium → 70–90%   |   Low → 0–70%

  Overall Score = 0.30 × T1 + 0.35 × T2 + 0.25 × T3 + 0.10 × T4
  PASS if Overall Score >= 65%

  IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
  ----------------------
  This validator is an AUTOMATED CONTRACT CHECKER — it verifies
  structural and lexical properties, not clinical correctness.
  A report can PASS all tiers and still contain:
    • Clinically incorrect dosages or treatment recommendations
    • Hallucinated patient details
    • Medically implausible reasoning that happens to use correct terms

  Human expert review is ALWAYS required before clinical use.
